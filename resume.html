Profil

I design load-bearing mechanical hardware and I close it with a calculation. For eight years that has meant a Creo model, a GD&T drawing, and a strength check by hand and in Ansys, against EN 81-20 and EN 1993. The parts are elevator shaft components and steel brackets: they must carry load, be weldable, and be ready for production. That is the work I can do on steel structures and mechanical installations.

Was ich einbringe / What I can do for the team

  • Turn a requirement and a standard into a concept, a detailed 3D model in Creo, and a manufacturing drawing with GD&T.
  • Check steel members, welds, and bolts to EN 1993 myself: hand calculation plus Ansys static, modal, and buckling, including the EN 1993-1-8 combined tension and shear check.
  • Check brackets and lift frames as loaded steel structures: model, drawing, and strength, then release to production.
  • Write the strength note, material callout, and release pack, and stay with the job through PDM. I lead six strength engineers who work this way.
  • Answer production from the drawing: sheet-metal tools, welding fixtures, and jigs, from three years in a chain factory.
  • Use Siemens NX on the project if that is the office system. I learned NX and SolidWorks in my degree. Creo and AutoCAD are what I use in industry.
