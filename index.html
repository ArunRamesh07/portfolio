About

Special Engineering Specialist at KONE in Chennai. I take elevator components from a requirement to a Creo model, a manufacturing drawing, and a strength check, using Ansys Workbench, hand calculation, or both.

I mentor a six-person strength team through to PDM release. A two-month assignment in Finland was on high-rise design and structural analysis.

Before KONE I designed chain hardware at Renold in Dindigul, including work for Renold Einbeck: drawings, press tools, jigs, sprockets, and the standards ISO 606, ASME B29.8, and BS 4116.

At work I use Creo 9 and AutoCAD. SolidWorks and Siemens NX are from my degree only. Codes I use are EN 81-20 and EN 1993, including bolts, welds, buckling, and beam reactions for isolation selection.

B.E. Mechanical Engineering, CGPA 7.5/10, Sri Ramakrishna Institute of Technology, Anna University, Coimbatore, 2014–2018.
